Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026 | Effective Date: August 2026

DPDP Act & GDPR Commitment: WebStarts ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to safeguarding your privacy in strict accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023/2026 of India and global data protection standards (GDPR). We collect zero non-essential tracking cookies and never sell your personal data.

1. Data We Collect

We only collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when submitting an inquiry through our contact forms or scheduling a discovery call:

  • Contact Details: First name, last name, business email address, phone number, and company name.
  • Project Specifications: Project briefs, technical requirements, estimated timelines, and budget ranges.
  • Technical Log Data: Minimal server logs (IP address, browser user-agent) strictly for security and anti-abuse verification.

2. Purpose of Data Processing

Under Section 4 and Section 6 of the India DPDP Act, your data is collected and processed exclusively for specified, lawful business purposes:

  • Evaluating and responding to your commercial project inquiries.
  • Preparing software architecture estimates, technical proposals, and service agreements.
  • Preventing fraudulent inquiries, automated spam attacks, and security vulnerabilities.

3. Zero-Cookie & Tracking Policy

Our website is engineered with a static architecture. We do not use third-party advertising cookies, cross-site trackers, or behavioral profiling scripts. Necessary session identifiers (such as Cloudflare bot defense tokens) are used solely for website security and operational performance.

4. Data Storage & Retention

All inquiry data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and stored in secure cloud systems with restricted role-based access. We retain lead and client data only for as long as necessary to fulfill business negotiations or comply with legal audit obligations.

5. Your Rights as a Data Principal

You have the right to request access, correction, updates, or complete erasure of your personal data at any time. To exercise these rights, please contact our Grievance Officer.

6. Data Protection & Grievance Redressal Officer

In compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, WebStarts has designated a Grievance Redressal Officer:

Designation: Data Protection & Grievance Officer
Entity: WebStarts LLP
Location: Indore, Madhya Pradesh 452010, India
Email: support@webstarts.in / grievance@webstarts.in
Response SLA: Within 48 business hours
